Your portal. Your preferences. Your privacy.

Effective October 18, 2026 · Applies to the Campuslit extension, version 0.2

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. Campuslit makes no requests to external servers, includes no remote fonts or scripts, and runs without any server of its own.

What it stores

Only your appearance choices: theme, the name and colors of the custom theme you design (if any), accent colour, font, text size, spacing, corner radius, motion and background options, the sidebar width you drag to, your Week or List time table choice, and whether styling is on for the CMS and the LMS. These live in the browser’s extension storage on your device. Campuslit does not use browser cloud sync. If assignment reminders are on (the default), it also keeps one short note in the same place: for each course, the titles and deadlines of assignments you have not submitted, so a reminder can appear on other LMS pages. It never leaves your device. Turning reminders off deletes it, and Settings has a button to forget it at any time.

What it does not collect

Apart from that optional reminder note, Campuslit does not record, store or send passwords, enrollment numbers, grades, attendance records, course content, fee details or browsing history. To redraw a few screens it reads figures the portal has already put on your page: your first name in the LMS greeting, your course list on the LMS dashboard, attendance hours and credit hours on the CMS dashboard and Attendance page (from which it works out how many hours you can miss), your assignment titles and deadlines on the LMS Assignments page, the names and addresses of the links in the portal's menu (for the optional quick search), and your class schedule on the CMS Time Table page. If you press Add to calendar there, the schedule becomes a calendar file in your browser that is saved as a download; nothing is uploaded. These are used in your browser’s memory only, shown back to you, and discarded when the page closes. We do not sell data, run analytics or show ads.

Why it can run on the portals

The extension runs only on pages under cms.bahria.edu.pk and lms.bahria.edu.pk. It checks the page address to choose the right look, applies your theme and responds to your changes. It does not read form values, intercept requests or alter what a form submits. It asks for the browser’s storage permission and nothing else.

What “stays in your browser” means

This promise covers Campuslit. The university’s websites still talk to the university and its services as they always do; Campuslit does not block that traffic, control the university’s own privacy practices or make an insecure site secure.

Export and import

Export saves a small file with only your appearance choices. Import reads only the file you pick. Neither uploads anything. If you share an exported file, that is your choice.

Your controls

Switch Campuslit off for everything or for one portal from its popup or settings, reset your preferences in settings, or remove the extension. Removing it normally deletes its stored data; files you exported must be deleted separately.

Fonts, files and updates

All fonts and files ship inside the extension package. The settings preview uses invented sample pages and never connects to your university account. If the extension’s permissions or data practices change, this page and the extension’s own privacy page will be updated before the change ships.

Independence

Campuslit is an independent student project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Bahria University. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT licence, so you can check every statement here.
