Privacy Policy

Campuslit is built so that your information stays with you. This page explains exactly what the extension and this website do with it.

At a glance

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Campuslit has no server of its own and makes no network requests.
  • It never reads your password or anything you type into a form on the portals.
  • It runs only on cms.bahria.edu.pk and lms.bahria.edu.pk, the student portals of Bahria University, and nowhere else.
  • What it keeps stays on your device: your appearance choices, an optional short note of open assignments so reminders work, and which tips you have seen.
  • You are in control. Switch it off, forget what it remembers, or remove it, and everything it saved goes with it.

1Who we are

Campuslit is an independent, open-source project made by Bahria University students. It is released by “Campuslit contributors” under the MIT Licence. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Bahria University, and it is not affiliated with it.

The code is public at github.com/ahmedammad1709/campuslit, so every statement on this page can be checked against it. To ask a question or report a concern, open an issue at github.com/ahmedammad1709/campuslit/issues.

2What the extension reads on the portals

To redraw a few screens, Campuslit reads figures the portal has already put on your page. It uses them in your browser’s memory to build the new screen and then lets them go. It does not copy them anywhere, except for the optional reminder note described in section 3.

WhatWhereUsed for
Your first nameLMS dashboardThe greeting, for example “Good morning, Ayesha”
Your course listLMS dashboardDrawing your courses as cards
Assignment titles, deadlines and whether each is submittedLMS Assignments pageThe Due soon card, the status under each deadline and the reminder
Attendance hours and credit hoursCMS dashboard and Attendance pageAttendance bars and the planner
Your class scheduleCMS Time Table pageThe week calendar, and the calendar file you can download
The names and addresses of the links in the portal’s menuBoth portalsQuick search

When you press Add to calendar, your schedule is turned into a calendar file inside your browser and saved as an ordinary download. Nothing is uploaded.

Campuslit does not read passwords, anything typed into form fields, or the contents of files you upload or download.

3What it stores on your device

Campuslit saves a small amount of information in your browser’s extension storage. It stays on this device. Campuslit does not use browser cloud sync, cookies or any other tracking technology.

ItemWhat it containsHow to remove it
Appearance settingsYour theme and any custom theme you design (its name and colours), font, text size, spacing, corner roundness, background and motion options, sidebar width, Week or List time table choice, semester length, and your on/off choices for the CMS, the LMS and each featureSettings › Privacy & data › Reset all settings
Assignment reminder note (optional, on by default)For each course: its name, the semester, and the titles and deadlines of assignments you have not submitted. It is used only to show a reminder on other LMS pages, and old entries expire on their ownTurn reminders off (this deletes it) or press Forget remembered assignments in Settings › Portals
Tip flagsA few yes/no facts: whether the welcome was shown, whether a tour replay was requested, and which one-time hints you dismissedSettings › Guide › Show the hints again, or Reset all settings

The settings file you can export contains appearance choices only. It holds no student information.

4What it never does

  • It never sends anything to us or to anyone else. The extension makes no network requests, its security policy blocks all outgoing connections, and each release is checked to confirm that no code can make them.
  • It never collects, sells, shares or profiles personal information, and it shows no ads.
  • It never changes your marks, your submissions or the data the university shows you. It changes only how pages look and adds helpers on top.
  • It never loads code or fonts from the internet. Everything it needs is inside the extension.

5Permissions

Campuslit asks the browser for as little as possible:

  • Storage, to remember the items in section 3 on this device.
  • Access to the two portals only (cms.bahria.edu.pk and lms.bahria.edu.pk), so it can restyle them. It cannot run on any other website.

It has no background process, no access to your tabs or browsing history, and no other permission.

6This website

This website describes Campuslit. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and it loads its fonts and images from itself. It is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address, the time and the page requested) to run and protect the service, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use those logs. Links to GitHub and other sites leave this website, and those sites have their own policies.

7The university portals

The CMS and LMS belong to Bahria University and are run by it. This policy covers Campuslit only. How the university handles your information on its portals is governed by its own rules, and Campuslit does not change that. Campuslit cannot promise anything about what the portals themselves send or store.

8Your control

  • The toolbar button has one switch for everything, and one each for the CMS and the LMS. Switch it off and the original pages return at once.
  • Settings can turn off assignment reminders, quick search, tips and the opening animation, and can forget the reminder note, show the hints again, or reset everything.
  • Removing the extension from your browser deletes everything it stored on your device.

9Changes and contact

If Campuslit’s handling of information ever changes, this page will be updated first and the effective date at the top will change. Every earlier version stays visible in the project’s public history on GitHub. If a change would involve collecting anything from users, it will be announced on this page and in the release notes before it ships.

Questions, concerns or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
