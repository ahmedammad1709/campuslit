Privacy & trust · Effective November 7, 2026 · Version 1.1.0

Your portal. Your preferences.
Your privacy.

The latest version of this policy is always at campuslit-bu.vercel.app/privacy.

Campuslit does not collect or transmit your personal data. Your appearance preferences stay in your browser.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking.
The extension sends nothing to external servers, includes no remote fonts or scripts, and operates without our own servers. Out of the box it makes no requests at all; only if you turn on notifications does it open pages on the two Bahria portals, using your own signed-in session.

What we store

We store only appearance preferences: your selected theme, the colors and name of the custom theme you design (if any), accent color, typography, text size, spacing, corner radius, motion and background choices, your semester length, and whether styling is enabled for CMS and LMS. These preferences use the browser's extension-local storage. We do not use browser cloud sync. If assignment reminders are on (the default), we also keep one short note in the same extension storage, on your device: for each course, the titles and deadlines of assignments you have not submitted, so a reminder can appear on other LMS pages. It is never sent anywhere. Turning reminders off deletes it, and Settings has a button to forget it at any time. It also remembers, as a few yes/no flags in the same place, whether the welcome has been shown and which one-time hints you have dismissed, so they do not repeat. Settings can turn tips off, show them again, or take the tour again.

What we do not collect

Apart from that optional reminder note and the tip flags, we do not record, store, or transmit passwords, enrollment numbers, grades, attendance records, course content, fee details, browsing history, or other student data. To redraw a few screens, the extension reads figures the portal has already put on your page (your first name in the LMS greeting, your course list in the LMS dashboard, the attendance hours and credit hours in the CMS dashboard and Attendance page (from which it works out how many hours you can miss), your assignment titles and deadlines on the LMS Assignments page, the names and addresses of the links in the portal's menu (for the optional quick search, never stored), and your class schedule on the CMS Time Table page, which is also what the optional Add to calendar button turns into a downloaded calendar file: built in your browser, never uploaded) in your browser's memory only, shows them back to you, and discards them when the page closes. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. We do not sell data, run analytics, or include advertising services.

Notifications (optional, off by default)

Campuslit asks you once, in the toolbar popup, whether to turn notifications on, and nothing is checked until you say yes. If you turn notifications on (there or in Settings), a small background process wakes every 1 to 8 hours (your choice) while your browser is open and opens a handful of pages on lms.bahria.edu.pk and cms.bahria.edu.pk, the same pages you would open yourself: your assignments, quizzes and lecture notes for each course, and the attendance on the CMS dashboard. It uses the session you are already signed in with, never signs in for you, and never sees your password. It compares what it finds with the last look, on your device, and shows a notification when something is new. It keeps a note in this browser of what it last saw (titles, marks and deadlines; your present and absent hours; which notices it has shown). Turning notifications off, pressing Forget what it remembers, or removing the extension deletes that note. It talks to no one but the two portals.

Why the extension can access the portals

The extension runs on pages under cms.bahria.edu.pk and lms.bahria.edu.pk to apply appearance styles. Its content script checks the page address to choose the portal and page type, applies theme variables, and responds to preference changes. It does not inspect form values, intercept requests, or alter the university's form submissions.

What “stays in your browser” means

This promise applies to this extension and its preferences. Bahria University's websites still communicate with the university and any services those websites use, just as they normally do. The extension does not block that traffic or control the university's privacy practices. It does not make an insecure connection secure or guarantee the security of the underlying portal.

Importing and exporting

Export creates a JSON file containing only your appearance preferences and a format version. The file is saved through your browser. Import reads only the file you explicitly select. Neither action uploads anything. If you share the exported file or save it to a cloud-synced folder, that sharing is under your control.

Your controls

You can disable styling globally or for either portal, reset preferences in Campuslit settings, or remove the extension. Reset removes saved preferences and returns to the default theme. Browser removal normally deletes extension-local storage; exported files must be deleted separately. Browser or operating-system backups are outside this extension's control.

Browser preview

If you open the standalone preview outside an installed extension, preferences are stored in that preview site's localStorage. The preview uses fictional sample content and does not connect to your university account.

Permissions and updates

The bundled Inter font is loaded locally from the extension package, never from a font service. The extension requests storage, alarms and notifications permissions and access to the two named portal domains only: its content scripts restyle those pages, and the optional background checker reads them. It has no access to your tabs, and the extension pages may connect only to those two domains. It does not request access to all websites, cookies, browser history, or downloads. Browser stores manage installation and update delivery under their own policies. Future changes to data handling will require a revised policy and extension release.

Independent project

Campuslit is an independent customization tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Bahria University. University names identify the websites the extension supports.
