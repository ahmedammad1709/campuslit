Assignment reminders

To remind you on other pages, Campuslit keeps a short note in this browser: the course, title and deadline of open assignments you have not submitted. It is never sent anywhere. Turning reminders off forgets it.

Experience

What Campuslit does with your data

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no requests to any server of ours.
  • Only your appearance choices are stored, in this browser, plus the short assignment-reminder note described under Portals (which you can turn off or forget).
  • Passwords and form fields are never read. A few screens are redrawn from figures the page already shows (your first name, courses, attendance, time table); they stay in memory and are never saved or sent.
