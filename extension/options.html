Assignment reminders

To remind you on other pages, Campuslit keeps a short note in this browser: the course, title and deadline of open assignments you have not submitted. It is never sent anywhere. Turning reminders off forgets it.

Experience

Notifications

How it works: while your browser is open and you are signed in to the LMS and CMS, Campuslit opens the same pages you would (your assignments, quizzes and lecture notes for each course, and the attendance on the CMS dashboard) and compares them with the last time. Everything stays in this browser. It never signs in for you and never sees your password. If your portal session ends, it says so once; sign in again to carry on. The CMS ends sessions quickly, so attendance alerts usually arrive when you next sign in to it; the LMS is checked in the background. Remember to save your changes.

Tips and tour

Campuslit remembers which tips you have dismissed as a few yes/no flags in this browser. Nothing else, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What Campuslit does with your data

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing sent to any server of ours. Only if you turn on notifications does Campuslit open pages on the two portals, with your own sign-in.
  • Only your appearance choices are stored, in this browser, plus the short assignment-reminder note described under Portals (which you can turn off or forget) and a few yes/no flags for the tips you have dismissed.
  • Passwords and form fields are never read. A few screens are redrawn from figures the page already shows (your first name, courses, attendance, time table); they stay in memory and are never saved or sent.
